# HelloGrowth CRM — 18-Cycle Master Research Plan

## Strategic Objective
Conduct a comprehensive, evidence-based, 18-cycle autonomous product gap analysis and competitor intelligence investigation of HelloGrowth CRM against enterprise, mid-market, specialized, and industry-vertical CRM platforms.

## Operating Principles
- **Read-Only Inspection:** No code commits, no database mutations, no config alteration.
- **Zero Hallucination:** Every claim traceable to live code, HTTP probe, or primary vendor documentation.
- **Deduplicated Master Backlog:** Every gap possesses a unique ID (`HGC-GAP-XXXX`), exact classification, opportunity score, and engineering acceptance criteria.
- **Cumulative Evidence Gathering:** Each cycle builds on previous discoveries without repetition.

---

## The 18 Research Cycles & Phased Execution

### Phase A: Product Understanding (Cycles 01–03)
- **Cycle 01: Product Architecture & Module Inventory:** Vite SPA shell, Supabase backend, Cloudflare edge, 367 views, 33 verticals, 53 webhooks, 14 API scopes, MCP server.
- **Cycle 02: CRM Sales Workflows:** End-to-end sales execution (Lead → Qualification → Deal → Proposal → Quote → Order → Invoice → Payment), stage gating, discount approvals.
- **Cycle 03: Communication & Marketing Workflows:** WhatsApp Cloud API, WebRTC VoIP dialer, 2-way SMS, Email deliverability, cadences, broadcasts, Meta Ads attribution.

### Phase B: Global Competitor Intelligence (Cycles 04–06)
- **Cycle 04: Enterprise CRM Competitors:** Salesforce Sales Cloud, Microsoft Dynamics 365, HubSpot Sales Hub, Zoho CRM.
- **Cycle 05: Mid-Market CRM Competitors:** Freshsales, Pipedrive, monday sales CRM, Insightly, Copper CRM.
- **Cycle 06: Emerging & Specialized Competitors:** Attio CRM, Close CRM, Creatio, Odoo, LeadSquared, Kylas CRM, GoHighLevel.

### Phase C: Industry-Specific Research (Cycles 07–10)
- **Cycle 07: Healthcare & Education:** Clinic EMR, doctor rosters, patient records, student admissions, installment fees, attendance, study abroad.
- **Cycle 08: Real Estate, Travel & Insurance:** Unit inventory hold locks, site visits, tour itineraries, policy lifecycles, IRDAI/ACORD compliance.
- **Cycle 09: Agri Inputs, Solar EPC & Manufacturing:** Field sales beat routes, rural distributor credit gates, solar rooftop BOQ, manufacturing work order capacity.
- **Cycle 10: Retail POS, Accounting & Digital Agencies:** Thermal receipt printing (WebUSB ESC/POS), offline IndexedDB POS caching, CA tax filings, agency white-label portals.

### Phase D: Advanced Product Analysis (Cycles 11–14)
- **Cycle 11: AI & Autonomous Agents:** Voice prospecting HUD, conversation intelligence, token audit logs, AI Quota Watcher, MCP server primitives.
- **Cycle 12: UI/UX & Mobile Usability:** 3-pane inbox responsiveness, table column pinning, illustrated empty states, PWA mobile field execution.
- **Cycle 13: Integrations & Webhooks:** 53 webhook events, Zapier/Make connectors, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, payment gateways (Razorpay/Stripe vs PayU).
- **Cycle 14: Security, Scalability & Performance:** Supabase RLS policies (currency vulnerability fix), compound B-tree indexes, tenant isolation.

### Phase E: Gap Validation (Cycles 15–17)
- **Cycle 15: Gap Deduplication & Status Classification:** Deduplication of all 45+ findings into confirmed categories.
- **Cycle 16: Competitive Differentiation & Moats:** Exploiting competitors' $100+ tier gates and Twilio markups with the $12 all-in-one moat.
- **Cycle 17: Implementation Roadmap & Technical Backlog:** Detailed 20-point engineering plans for P0 and P1 priorities.

### Phase F: Final Validation (Cycle 18)
- **Cycle 18: Independent Audit, Verification & Deliverables Finalization:** Complete verification of all artifacts, CSV matrices, and Excel workbook.
